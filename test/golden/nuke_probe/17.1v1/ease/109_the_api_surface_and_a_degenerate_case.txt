Every one straight except the step type, which holds its
value here as it does on a three-key curve. So the
degeneracy is specific to the types that SHAPE a segment:
with no third key to derive a tangent from, and endpoint
tangents ignored (case 117), there is nothing left for a
cubic to be but the chord. Use three keys.
