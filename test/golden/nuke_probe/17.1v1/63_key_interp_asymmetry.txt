If 1=STEP, 2=LINEAR, 3=CUBIC then the field is simply the
enum plus one and there is no mystery - just an undocumented
offset. 256 would then be a separate 'unset' sentinel bit.

If one type governs BOTH sides of a Nuke key, then AE's
in=LINEAR/out=HOLD has no Nuke equivalent and .rbj can stay
single-valued for the Nuke direction - but AE->AE round trips
through .rbj would still lose it. That is the Q9 tradeoff.
